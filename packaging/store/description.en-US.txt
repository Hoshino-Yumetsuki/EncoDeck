Manage your Bluetooth earbuds right from Windows — no need to pick up your phone just to switch noise cancellation.

EncoDeck brings the everyday settings of your earbuds' phone app to the PC: check battery, switch noise control, adjust sound, and manage touch gestures. It lives quietly in the system tray and is one click away when you need it.

OPPO and OnePlus earbuds are the primary focus, with partial support for earbuds from several other major brands. Available features vary by model; EncoDeck shows only the settings your earbuds actually support.

BATTERY & STATUS
• Left, right and case battery; a single level for headphones
• Charging, wear status and firmware version (model dependent)
• Low-battery alerts; stale case readings are labelled "last read"

NOISE CONTROL
• Noise cancelling / Transparency / Off / Adaptive, plus ANC strength
• Stays in sync when you change modes with gestures or your phone
• Global hotkeys to switch modes, even in full-screen games

SOUND
• Built-in EQ presets; custom EQ on supported models
• Spatial audio and head tracking, dynamic bass, low-latency game mode

EARBUD SETTINGS
• Touch gestures, wear detection, prompt volume
• Find my earbuds, ear-tip fit test
• Dual-device connection management

WINDOWS INTEGRATION
• Tray quick panel with light and dark themes
• On Windows 11, a panel docks above Quick Settings (Win + A)
• Connection pop-up with device card and animation; stays quiet in full screen
• Product images or a rotatable 3D model on the home page
• Runs in the background, starts with Windows, updates through the Microsoft Store

Pair and connect your earbuds in Windows Bluetooth settings first. Some product images and animations are downloaded on first use.

EncoDeck is an open-source, unofficial tool and is not affiliated with any earbud brand. Apart from the OPPO Enco X4, most models have not yet been tested on real hardware — feedback is welcome on GitHub: https://github.com/TIANLI0/EncoDeck
